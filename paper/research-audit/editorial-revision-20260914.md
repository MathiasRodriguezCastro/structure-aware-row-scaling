# Editorial revision — 14 September 2026

Reframing of the manuscript around: scaling changes the representation; attribution requires
matched controls; the mechanism is simpler than the metadata explanation; residual budgets are an
invariant contract; solver outcomes are measured and verified separately.

## New evidence added

- **Fixed-basis conditioning audit, all 306 dispatch models** (`results-revision/research-audit/fixed-basis-final-20260914/`).
  Driver `scripts/research/fixed_basis_campaign.py` (reuses `audit_fixed_basis.py`), analysis
  `scripts/research/analyze_fixed_basis.py`, verification `scripts/research/verify_fixed_basis.py`.
  Reference basis: Base LP relaxation, HiGHS 1.15.1 simplex, one thread (deterministic, license-free).
  - GM-local policies (Flat-GM, Role-Hybrid, SA-Mat) lower kappa_1 on 306/306 models; median ratios
    2.4e-7 (Simple, n=204), 7.3e-9 (SG-Ter-Mer, n=68), 3.5e-7 (Full, n=34).
  - Flat-L2: median ratio 1.49-1.57, improves 44/306. Every row has max|a| >= 1 in all 306 models, so no
    L2 factor exceeds one; Flat-L2 leaves 58-64% of rows unscaled vs 22-30% for Flat-GM.
  - SA-Mat and Role-Hybrid exports byte-identical on 306/306; they differ from Flat-GM only on the
    1,440 `falla_tramo` rows per SG-Ter-Mer/Full model (146,880 rows in total). Coupling rows receive
    identical factors, so this identity does not test ownership.
  - Verification: 1,530/1,530 bases rebuilt exactly from LP text; equilibrated-route estimates within
    1e-6 for 1,512 records and within 23% for all; estimator seeds within a factor 2.4.
  - The archived fixed-incumbent medians (1.2e-3 / 3.6e-4 / 0.41) are not used as evidence: no exports,
    basis identities or binary hash were retained. Magnitudes depend on the reference basis.
- **Precision check of the fixed-basis values** (`fixed-basis-final-20260914/precision-check/`,
  `scripts/research/verify_fixed_basis_precision.py`, SI Table S2). Stress sample fixed before
  computation: per class largest Base estimate, largest Flat-GM/Base ratio, seeded random (seed
  20260915); 8 models (Full's first two coincide), 24 bases (Base, Flat-GM, Flat-L2).
  - Certified lower bound on kappa_1: exact rational residuals + ||B^-1||_1 >= ||x||_1/||Bx||_1.
  - A posteriori upper bound over all columns of B^-1 (floating point with explicit rounding terms);
    largest residual bound 6.0e-3.
  - Enclosure widths: Base <= 6.0e-3, Flat-L2 <= 4.8e-3, Flat-GM <= 2.0e-5. Flat-GM/Base ratio bounds
    agree with the estimates to 2.1e-5. 23/24 estimates inside their enclosure; Full instance046
    Flat-L2 estimate is 2.4x below its certified lower bound (estimator underestimation, against
    Flat-L2). Rerun with the final script reproduced results.json byte for byte.
- Title changed to "Pre-Export Row Scaling for Mixed-Integer Programs: Mechanism Attribution and
  Residual-Budget Contracts" (main, SI, README files, CITATION.cff, .zenodo.json, cover-letter.txt).
- Discussion states once why GCD-floor (192/192) does not make budgets redundant.
- **Paired analysis of the stress experiment** (`budget-final/paired-discordance.json`,
  `paper/tables/budget-paired.tex`, generated by `analyze_budget_experiment.py`).
  - Budget-GM vs GM: 41-0 (HiGHS), 12-0 (Gurobi). Budget-dyadic vs Dyadic-GM: 19-0, 13-0.
  - Budget-only vs Base: 24-0 (HiGHS), 1-6 (Gurobi). GM vs Base: 0-26, 0-16.
  - Budget-only vs Budget-GM: all 10 discordant configurations at e in {6,9}, where the two factors
    differ by exactly one ulp (inward rounding of the binding endpoint). The 191 vs 182 HiGHS gap is an
    acceptance sensitivity, not an effect of coefficient centering.
