# OMS manuscript preparation

The current scientific snapshot is `v1.3.14-oms-submission` (17 October 2026).
The manuscript has two main contributions: numerical mechanism attribution and
accuracy-aware row scaling. Saved results retain adverse outcomes, abstentions,
unknown exact refinements and the limits of bound certification.

The OMS copy uses the publisher's Interact class and numerical Q bibliography
style. The publisher files and their hashes are documented in
[TEMPLATE_PROVENANCE.md](TEMPLATE_PROVENANCE.md). The NLM template uses the same
class after newline normalization; its isolated comparison preserved all 32
bibliographic keys and compiled to 36 pages. The required journal-specific
bibliography style remains subject to the submission portal.

The PDF contains a 193-word abstract, six keywords and four MSC 2020 codes. The
same codes occur in MSC 2010. The manuscript margins exceed the secondary guide's
25 mm threshold. The official journal instructions were inaccessible during
preparation; general publisher guidance is not treated as their replacement.

sources in `paper/`. The cover letter is one page. Supplementary Material 1 has

in Supplementary Material 2. See the
[submission checklist](../submission/oms/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md) and
