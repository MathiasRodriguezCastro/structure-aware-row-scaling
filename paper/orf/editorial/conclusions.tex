\subsection{Conclusions}
The contribution is a reproducible model-generation procedure connecting
original-unit precision requirements to admissible row factors, checked export
and independently verified returned points. Established transfer relations
become explicit selection or abstention decisions, with allowances for integer
rounding and finite export.

Controlled comparisons distinguish conditioning from solver effort, identify a
kernel-confounded metadata interpretation and expose an inactive coupling stage.
Selected stress tests recover primal accuracy; batch scheduling verifies transfer
without an additional benefit over matched L2. The 24-scenario study improves
work and time against Base. In the 3,840-assignment confirmation, the primary
Budget-L2/L2 time intervals contain one and Gurobi process cost increases.
All policies meet physical budgets in both prospective samples. CPLEX acceptance
is sensitive to tiny written-bound residues under the unchanged registered rule.

For implementation, retain original units, record admissibility decisions,
audit export and returned points, and compare candidate quality and complete
cost. The resulting software makes precision choices and their computational
consequences traceable.
