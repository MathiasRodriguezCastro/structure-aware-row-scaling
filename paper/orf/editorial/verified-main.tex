\paragraph{A common verified-candidate target}
A follow-up on the same 24 scenarios, with three new seeds, tests physical
budgets and candidate quality jointly (\cref{tab:orf-verified-candidates}).
The primary target requires cost at most
$U_{\rm ref}+0.001\max\{1,|U_{\rm ref}|\}$, using an independently checked
feasible reference, not a certified optimum. The audit also checks exact
rounded-point bounds, integer distances and remaining-row backward errors.
Callbacks save candidates; rational verification runs after solving.
Thus verified availability includes waiting for solver termination and auditing
the candidate prefix, rather than measuring an online stopping rule.
