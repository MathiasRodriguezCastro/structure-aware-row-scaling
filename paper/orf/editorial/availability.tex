\section*{Data and software availability}
Code and stored results are supplied in Online Resource~2 for the local snapshot
\path{v1.4.0-orf-submission}, dated 18 October 2026. The repository is
\url{https://github.com/MathiasRodriguezCastro/structure-aware-row-scaling}.
This is an editorial adaptation of the independently audited scientific archive
\path{v1.3.17-oms-submission}; no new optimization experiments are introduced.
Online Resource~2 includes that exact archived code/data ZIP, its validation record,
the current editable manuscript and Online Resource~1, and a checksum-based
inheritance record. Historical internal filenames are retained for traceability.

The public experimental baseline \path{v1.2-coap-submission} is archived at
\doi{10.5281/zenodo.23145294}, with concept DOI
\doi{10.5281/zenodo.20648949}. These identifiers describe the earlier baseline,
not the later experiments or this submission snapshot. The current snapshot has
no separate Zenodo version DOI.

Online Resource~1 gives proofs, protocols and reproduction instructions. Saved-result
replay requires no commercial optimizer license; it checks the supplied exports,
logs, points, rational witnesses, unit conversions and summaries. It also reconstructs
the 120 additional named bases. Rebuilding all 1,530 historical bases additionally
requires the baseline's \texttt{fixed-basis-exports.tar.zst} (337 MB compressed,
5.18 GB uncompressed). Fresh commercial-solver experiments require the corresponding
licenses. Online Resource~2 gives the commands and distinguishes replay from fresh runs.
