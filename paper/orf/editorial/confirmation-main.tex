\paragraph{Larger fixed-policy confirmation}
A subsequent frozen study compares four policies on 160 models: 40 historically
generated parents, three dispatch formulations and four horizons, with three
new seeds and two solvers. All 3,840 assignments and 480 independent block
replays finish; 24 assignments of an independently infeasible model remain
diagnostic. Table~\ref{tab:orf-large-confirmation} reports the primary comparison
on the other 3,816 assignments, using a common verified feasible cost reference
and a 0.1\% target. Native stopping gaps are also 0.1\%; caps are fixed from
Base-only resource calibration before policy comparisons. These results are
not pooled with the earlier study, which uses a different stopping gap.
