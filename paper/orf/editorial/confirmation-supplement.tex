\section{Larger fixed-policy confirmation}
\label{sec:orf-large-confirmation}
The complete execution protocol, frozen plans and provenance accompany
\path{confirmation-evidence-20261022.tar.xz} in Online Resource~2. The study
uses 14 Simple, 13 SG-Ter-Mer and 13 Full parent inputs, four horizons
(24, 72, 168, 360 hours), seeds 401/457/503, Gurobi 13.0.2 and CPLEX 22.1.2.0,
and four policies per solver. Simple, SG-Ter-Mer and Full are formulations
within the hydrothermal application, not independent application families.
Their inputs recombine historical components; prior exposure to scaling
policies is documented. No operational independence or observational holdout
is claimed. The Full continuation function remains fixed across horizons,
so the longer horizon is a dimensional sensitivity, not a recalibrated forecast.

\paragraph{Frozen execution and reference acquisition}
The study runs one fresh MIP process at a time with four threads and 8 GiB.
Each model/seed block keeps all eight solver/policy calls on one node, using a
fixed shuffled order. Policies are Base, L2, Budget-L2 and L2-NF3 for Gurobi or
L2-NumEmphasis for CPLEX; these two numerical controls are not equated. Native
presolve/internal scaling remain automatic. Feasibility and integer tolerances
are $10^{-6}$ and $10^{-9}$; the requested gap is 0.1\% for all four policies.
Caps are 60 s except Full/360/CPLEX and SG-Ter-Mer/360/Gurobi (120 s), and
Full/360/Gurobi (300 s). Base-only calibration fixed these resources before
comparisons. The last stratum did not reach the operational Base-stop target;
its finite-limit censoring is retained, not repaired by changing the sample.

Common-reference acquisition is separate: 171 Base MIP calls and 171 rational
LP attempts yield 149 exactly feasible references, ten unknowns and one
independently infeasible model. Ten additional feasibility LPs, with zero new
MIPs, recover the unknown references. Thus 159 references are exactly feasible;
none is assumed a certified MILP optimum. Calibration, the disjoint 256-call
resource pilot, reference acquisition, installation and queue time are excluded
from the comparative process clocks and identified separately in the archive.
The complete 24 assignments of the infeasible Full/106/168-hour model remain
diagnostic. The remaining 3,816 assignments form the common eligible mask.

An initial attempt stopped after 624 assignments, including twelve pre-solver
export-hash failures. A loader diagnostic traced the one-ulp factor change to
global CPLEX preloading. Normal library-path loading reproduced the frozen
exports and retained the unrestricted license. The entire attempt was excluded
from comparison and retained as infrastructure history; all 3,840 assignments
were repeated uniformly without changing factors, seeds, order or budgets.
An earlier shell-only admission failure and the resource pilot's BLAS-kernel
correction are recorded separately. The archive supplies the final campaign,
pilot and reference raw data, plus provenance and manifests for these excluded
attempts; their separate raw infrastructure archives remain outside this bundle.

\paragraph{Acceptance and the primary endpoint}
The physical map uses 0.01 MW and 100 m\textsuperscript{3} for its mapped rows,
before and after integer rounding. Unmapped Rio Negro activation rows acquire
no physical contract. Acceptance also requires integer distances at most
$10^{-9}$, exact rounded-point bounds and remaining-row relative backward error
at most $10^{-6}$. Every explicitly written finite bound is retained, including
values that a native reader treats as an infinity sentinel. These rules are
fixed before results and are not relaxed for tiny violations.

The cost threshold is
$C_{\rm ref}+\operatorname{binary64}(0.001)\max\{1,|C_{\rm ref}|\}$,
evaluated exactly from original coefficients and rounded integer coordinates.
Callbacks retain all candidate vectors. Verification occurs after solving:
availability includes termination and auditing the candidate prefix, rather
than an online certified stopping rule. PAR2 uses measured verified availability
on success and twice the declared solver cap plus measured preparation on
failure. Complete-process time additionally includes persistence. Work is
solver-specific, finite-limit censored and not an equal-quality certificate.
No Gurobi work/CPLEX tick pooling is performed.

The primary comparison is Budget-L2/L2 separately by solver. The registered
100,000-resample bootstrap draws complete parents, keeping all horizons,
seeds and the diagnostic mask together; seed 2026102021 and deterministic
solver/stratum offsets are supplied. Parent totals and counts preserve the
observed weights when eligible blocks have different sizes. Common-success
intervals retain parents with no common successes in the sampling frame and
count undefined replicas instead of imputing them. Intervals are marginal,
conditional on this historically generated population, and not evidence of
equivalence or external validity. The independent multiplicity-based replay
reconstructs all 48 comparisons and 80 intervals from 480 checked block reports.

\paragraph{Physical precision, domains and bound applicability}
All 3,816 eligible final points meet the mapped physical budgets. Simple's L2
and Budget-L2 exports are identical, so their timing differences cannot measure
an effect of changing contractual factors. Across the eligible sample the
contract supplies no additional physical-budget recovery. The global PAR2
ratios are 0.995 for Gurobi and 1.000 for CPLEX, with both intervals containing
one. This establishes neither general acceleration nor equivalence. Gurobi's
complete-process ratio is 1.079 [1.032,1.133]; the slight CPLEX work decrease
is secondary and does not establish an improvement in the primary endpoint.

CPLEX's exact-domain failures are all positive written-bound residues, ranging

not evidence of valid bounds. Base provides 477 applicable contrasts per
solver, with two Gurobi and thirteen CPLEX contradictions. The acceptance checks
and the absence of an applicable contradiction detector do not certify MILP
optimality. All 48 control/stratum comparisons, native gaps and time-limit
outcomes remain in the machine-readable archive; no favorable strata are used
