\item \emph{A verifiable model-generation procedure.} The practical contribution
is the integration of established residual-transfer ideas into explicit software
decisions: declare original-unit budgets, select admissible factors or report
incompatibility, check export, and independently verify returned points. The
selector combines coefficient limits, constrained centering, unit re-emission
invariance, integer-rounding allowances and finite-export error reserves.
Its guarantees are conditional on the actual exported residual
(\cref{sec:contract,sec:implementation}).
\item \emph{An auditable computational evaluation.} Fixed-basis, kernel-matched
and equilibration controls separate representation changes from solver outcomes,
including an apparent metadata advantage explained by the kernel. Operational
and prospective tests retain failures, candidate quality and complete-process
cost; the larger fixed-policy confirmation tests the incremental value over L2
with whole-parent uncertainty and explicit acceptance sensitivity
(\cref{sec:identifiability,subsec:results-ruiz,subsec:orf-prospective,subsec:orf-large-confirmation}).
