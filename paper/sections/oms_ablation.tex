\subsection{Complementary check of applied final-policy transformations}
\label{subsec:results-ablation}
The six-model stage check in Table~\ref{tab:oms-ablation} records which transformations
were actually applied by the final Role-Hybrid implementation. Its factor audit checks
that the local and coupling stages have disjoint nonunit supports and compose to the
full export. This is an implementation check on the stated subset.

On all six models, every coupling candidate is suppressed by the final identity band:
Coupling-only is byte-identical to Base, and Local-only is byte-identical to Role-Hybrid.
The full policy's effort is therefore reproduced by its local stage, with total-time
differences between identical exports reflecting measurement variation. This identifies
the active transformation in this guarded implementation; it does not test the benefit
of an active coupling transformation or establish a general local-stage dominance result.
